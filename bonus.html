How Quotex Bonuses Work

  1. Choose the bonus — in the deposit window, select an available offer or type a promo code.
  2. Deposit — the bonus is calculated as a percentage of the amount you pay.
  3. Trade — your balance now shows deposit + bonus. Every trade counts towards the turnover target.
  4. Unlock — once turnover is completed, the bonus funds become withdrawable along with any profits.
Important

While a bonus is active, requesting a withdrawal before completing turnover may cancel the bonus — and in some cases profits linked to it. Always read the current bonus terms on the platform.

Turnover Requirements, With a Real Example

"Turnover" (also called trading volume or wagering) is the total value of trades you must place before bonus funds can be withdrawn. On deposit bonuses it is commonly set as a multiple of the bonus amount — the exact multiplier is stated in the offer terms.

Here's an illustration using a 35× multiplier on the bonus amount (check the actual figure for your offer):

Your deposit
$100
Bonus (+30%)
$30
Starting balance
$130
Turnover multiplier
35×
Trading volume required
$1,050

$1,050 of volume means, for example, 210 trades of $5 each. Every trade you place counts, whether it wins or loses. The more volume required, the more trades you'll place — and the more exposure to risk you take on.

Terms to check before activating any bonus

  • Multiplier: how many times the bonus (or deposit + bonus) must be traded.
  • Time limit: whether unused bonus funds expire.
  • Withdrawal rules: what happens to the bonus if you withdraw early.
  • Eligible assets: whether all trades count fully towards turnover.
  • Cancellation: whether you can remove the bonus via support if you change your mind.

Tips to Get the Most Value From a Quotex Bonus

Size the deposit to your plan

Never deposit more just to reach a bigger bonus tier. Bonus value only matters if the deposit itself fits your risk budget.

Calculate turnover first

Work out the trading volume before you click "activate". If it would require stakes larger than 2% of your balance, skip it.

Keep stakes consistent

Hitting turnover quickly with big trades is the fastest way to lose the balance. Small, steady stakes work better.

It's fine to say no

Many experienced traders trade without bonuses so they can withdraw freely. A bonus is optional — not a requirement.
