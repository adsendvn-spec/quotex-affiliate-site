2

Verify Your Identity (KYC)

Verification protects your account and is required before any withdrawal. Doing it early avoids delays when you want your money out.

  • Proof of identity: Aadhaar card, PAN card, passport or driving licence.
  • Selfie: a clear photo of you, sometimes holding the ID.
  • Profile details: full name and date of birth exactly as on your document.
What you'll see

Under Profile → Verification, an upload area with document-type options. Status changes from "Not verified" to "Pending" and then a green "Verified" badge, usually within 24 hours.

Tip

Use good lighting and make sure all four corners of the document are visible. Blurry photos are the number-one reason for rejected KYC.

3

Practise on the $10,000 Demo Account

This is the most important step, and the one most beginners rush. The demo account uses live market prices but virtual money, so you can make mistakes for free.

  • Learn where each control is: asset selector, stake, expiry, Up/Down buttons and trade history.
  • Add one or two indicators — for example a moving average and RSI — and observe how they behave.
  • Write down simple rules (when you enter, how much you stake) and follow them for at least 50 demo trades.
  • Track your win rate. Remember that at an 85% payout you need to win roughly 54% of trades just to break even.

5

Place Your First Live Trade

  1. Pick a liquid asset you practised on, such as EUR/USD or gold.
  2. Check the payout percentage shown next to the asset.
  3. Set a small stake — ideally 1–2% of your balance (e.g. $1 on a $50 account).
  4. Choose the expiry time you tested on demo.
  5. Press Up if you expect price to finish higher, Down if lower.
  6. Review the result in the Trades panel and log it in your journal.
What you'll see

A countdown timer on the chart with a horizontal line marking your entry price. The line turns green when the trade is in profit and red when it's not, until expiry.

Learn from others' mistakes

Trading Tips for Quotex Beginners

Habits that separate careful traders from those who burn through deposits quickly.

Set a daily loss limit

Decide in advance the maximum you'll lose in a day — and stop when you hit it, no exceptions.

Never chase losses

Doubling the stake after a loss (martingale) can empty an account in a handful of trades.

Avoid ultra-short expiries early

Very short trades behave close to random. Longer expiries give analysis more time to work.

Trade during liquid hours

For forex, the London–New York overlap (roughly 6:30–10:30 pm IST) usually offers cleaner moves.

Keep a trading journal

Record asset, time, reason and result. Patterns in your own mistakes become obvious within weeks.

Ignore "guaranteed" signals

Telegram groups promising sure-shot wins are a common scam. No one can guarantee profits.
