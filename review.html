Platform Features: A Deep Dive

Quotex built its own trading terminal rather than licensing a white-label product, and it shows. The interface is clean, responsive and notably light on system resources — we ran it on a three-year-old Android phone over mobile data without noticeable lag.

Charting and analysis

You get four chart types (area, candlestick, bars and Heikin-Ashi), timeframes from seconds to a day, and a toolbox of roughly 30 indicators including RSI, MACD, Stochastic, Bollinger Bands, Alligator and several moving averages. Drawing tools cover trend lines, horizontal levels, Fibonacci retracement and channels. Up to four charts can be viewed side-by-side on desktop.

Order execution

Trades are placed with two buttons — Up or Down — after choosing the stake and the expiry. A "pending trade" feature lets you queue an order that opens only when price reaches a level you set, which is handy for disciplined, rules-based trading. In our tests, orders opened instantly with no requotes.

Social and signals features

The platform shows a sentiment bar (what percentage of traders are betting up versus down) and an in-built signals panel. We'd treat both as information, not instructions — neither replaces your own analysis, and no signal is reliably profitable.

Account levels

Quotex rewards higher deposits with status tiers that add a few percentage points to payouts and unlock perks such as more simultaneous trades or priority withdrawals. Don't deposit more just to unlock a tier — the extra payout rarely compensates for the extra risk.

Mobile experience

With most Indian traders on mobile, this matters. The Android app and mobile web version mirror the desktop features, including indicators and pending trades. iPhone users can trade through the mobile browser and pin it to the home screen; it works well, though without some native-app conveniences.

How to deposit with UPI

  1. Log in and tap Deposit in the top bar.
  2. Select UPI and enter the amount (minimum $10 equivalent).
  3. Scan the QR code or approve the collect request in your UPI app.
  4. Your balance usually updates within a minute or two.

How to withdraw

  1. Complete identity verification (KYC) first — unverified accounts can't withdraw.
  2. Open Withdrawal, choose the same method you deposited with where possible.
  3. Enter your UPI ID or bank details carefully and submit.
  4. Track the request status under Transactions.
Tax reminder

Gains from trading may be taxable in India, and foreign remittances can attract TCS under LRS rules. Keep records of every deposit and withdrawal and consult a qualified tax professional.

Is Quotex Regulated? The Honest Answer

No — Quotex is not regulated by SEBI, the RBI or any top-tier financial regulator. It is incorporated offshore. Earlier, the company advertised membership of an international dispute-resolution body, but such memberships are not equivalent to licensing by a government regulator.

The Reserve Bank of India maintains an Alert List of entities that are not authorised to deal in forex or operate electronic trading platforms for forex transactions. Several popular binary/digital options brands, including Quotex, have appeared on this list. Indian residents should understand that using such platforms carries legal uncertainty and that Indian authorities offer no investor protection or grievance mechanism for them.

What this means in practice

If something goes wrong — a disputed trade or a delayed withdrawal — you cannot complain to SEBI or the RBI. Only deposit amounts you are fully prepared to lose, and read our FAQ on whether Quotex is legal in India.

On the positive side, in our testing the platform processed every withdrawal we requested, applied two-factor authentication options, and used SSL encryption across the site. These are good signs but are not a substitute for regulation.
